Your first spa day in Georgia: what to expect

Never been to a spa, or just never sure what you’re supposed to do once you’re there? It’s genuinely easy. Here’s everything a first-timer wants to know.

What to book

If you’re not sure, start with a 60-minute Swedish massage or a signature facial — both are gentle, standard, and a good read on whether you like the place. Couples can book a side-by-side massage. For skin concerns like fine lines or pigmentation, you want a med spa instead.

Before you go

Arrive 10–15 minutes early to fill out a short intake form and change. Wear something comfortable; you’ll be given a robe and a private space. Skip a heavy meal right before a massage, and flag any injuries, allergies, or pregnancy when you book — it changes what’s safe to do.

Tipping and cost

For massage and facials, 15–20% is customary unless gratuity is already included. Med-spa treatments (injectables, laser) generally aren’t tipped. Ask about first-visit offers — many spas run new-client discounts.

Getting the most out of it

Put your phone away, drink water after a massage, and tell your therapist if pressure is too light or too firm — they’d much rather adjust. Found one you love? You can save it and book again.
