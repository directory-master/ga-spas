The best med spas in Georgia (2026)

A med spa sits between a day spa and a doctor’s office: medical-grade treatments — Botox and fillers, laser hair removal, microneedling, chemical peels, IV therapy — delivered under clinical oversight. Because the results (and the risks) are higher, who you book matters more than at a relaxation spa. These are the highest-rated med spas across Georgia right now.

What makes a good med spa

Look for a licensed medical director on site, clear before-and-after expectations, and a consult before any injectable. Strong, consistent reviews are the best public signal that a clinic delivers — which is exactly what this ranking rewards.
